Effective October 6, 2026 · Version 1.0

Cleo is a point-of-sale and business management service made by Aeso Technologies Inc. ("Aeso", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Cleo, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the rights you have. It covers the Cleo Terminal, Owner Dashboard, Back Office, Cleo Cloud and the Cleo website.

We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). We collect only what Cleo needs to work, keep it only as long as we need it, and never sell it.

## 1. Who this policy covers, and our role

- **People who sign in to Cleo**: business owners, managers, accountants and staff. For your account, Aeso decides how your data is used, so we are the personal information controller.
- **Customers of businesses that use Cleo.** When a café, store or service shop records its own customers in Cleo, that business is the personal information controller of that data. Aeso processes it only on the business's instructions, as its personal information processor, under the data processing terms in our [Terms and Conditions](../Frontend/legal/terms.html). If you are a customer of a business that uses Cleo, ask that business first; we help businesses answer these requests.
- **Visitors to the Cleo website.**

## 2. Personal data we collect

### When you have a Cleo account

- **Account details:** your name, work email, mobile number, role and the branches you work in. We store your password only as a salted hash, never in readable form.
- **Google or Facebook sign-in:** if you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook", the provider shares your name, email address and profile picture link with your permission. We never receive your Google or Facebook password, and we never post on your behalf.
- **Security records:** your two-step sign-in setting, whether your backup codes have been used (the codes themselves are stored as hashes), sign-in times, devices, IP addresses, and failed sign-in and PIN attempts.
- **Work records the business keeps about you:** your staff PIN (stored as a hash), time-clock entries, and the sales, voids, refunds, discounts and approvals linked to your name. Businesses use these for their audit trail, shift reconciliation and staff reports.
- **Billing details:** your plan, invoices and billing contact. Card details are collected and stored by our payment provider, not by Aeso. We only see the card brand, the last four digits and the expiry date.
- **Messages you send us**, such as support requests.

### Data a business records in Cleo (we process it for the business)

- **Customer profiles:** name, mobile number, email, birthday, home branch, TIN and address for invoices, loyalty points, purchase history, charge account limits and balances, and the date the customer agreed to be recorded.
- **Statutory discount details:** for senior citizen, PWD, National Athletes and Coaches, and Solo Parent discounts, the ID type, ID number and name on the ID. The laws on these discounts (Republic Act Nos. 9994, 10754, 10699 and 11861) and the BIR's sales books require businesses to record them.
- **Delivery orders** from GrabFood or foodpanda, when the business connects them: the customer's first name or initial and what they ordered.
- **E-receipts:** the mobile number or email a receipt is sent to.
- **Expense receipt photos**, which can show names.

### Collected automatically

- **Device and app details:** device type, operating system, app version, which branch a terminal is registered to, sync status and error reports.
- **Data kept on your device:** so a terminal can keep selling without internet, Cleo stores what it needs on the device: the catalog, prices, the staff list and sales that haven't synced yet. It also remembers your preferences, such as light or dark mode, the mode this device opens in and your table layouts. This data stays on the device until it syncs, until you sign out or unregister the terminal, or until you clear the app's data.
- **Cookies and similar storage:** only what is needed to keep you signed in, remember your settings and keep Cleo secure. We don't use advertising cookies and we don't share data with advertisers.

## 3. Why we use personal data

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| To run Cleo for you: accounts, sign-in, syncing terminals, receipts, reports and support | Our contract with you or your business |
| To keep Cleo and your business secure: two-step sign-in, fraud checks, the audit trail | Our legitimate interest in a secure service, and legal obligations |
| To help businesses meet BIR, tax and statutory discount rules | Legal obligations of the business, which Cleo is built to support |
| To bill your subscription | Our contract with you |
| To send service messages: outages, security notices, changes to these policies | Our contract with you, and our legitimate interest |
| To improve Cleo, using combined or de-identified usage data | Our legitimate interest |
| To tell you about other Aeso products | Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |

Some details are sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act, such as PWD status and government-issued ID numbers. Cleo processes them only because the discount laws above require the business to record them, and only the roles that need them can see them.

## 4. Who we share personal data with

- **Service providers** that host Cleo, process payments (QR Ph and cards), deliver SMS and email, and support our help desk. They act on our instructions under written contracts that require them to protect the data.
- **Google and Meta (Facebook)**, only when you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to what you share with them.
- **Services a business connects**, such as GrabFood and foodpanda, for orders and menus. The business decides whether to connect them and can disconnect them at any time.
- **The business you work for and its authorized users.** For example, an owner can see the sales and approvals linked to each staff member.
- **Government authorities**, such as the BIR, the NPC or the courts, when the law requires it.
- **A buyer or successor**, if Aeso is part of a merger or acquisition. We will tell you before your data moves to a new company and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Where data is stored

Cleo Cloud stores data with established hosting providers. Some providers may process data outside the Philippines. When they do, we stay responsible for that data under the Data Privacy Act and require the same level of protection by contract. You can ask us for the current list of service providers and hosting locations at [privacy@aeso.ph](mailto:privacy@aeso.ph).

## 6. How long we keep data

- **Account data:** while your account is active. If your business cancels Cleo, your data stays available to export for 90 days, and then we delete or anonymize it, except where the law requires us to keep it.
- **Sales, invoices, the electronic journal and the audit trail:** for as long as Philippine tax law requires the business to keep them. We keep them for the business during that time, or hand them over in an export.
- **Security logs:** up to 12 months.
- **Backups:** backups are replaced on a rolling basis and kept for up to 30 days.
- **Marketing consent:** until you withdraw it.

When a business removes a customer from Cleo, their profile and contact details are erased. Past invoices keep only the buyer details that tax law requires.

## 7. How we protect personal data

- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Passwords, staff PINs and backup codes are stored only as hashes.
- Two-step sign-in for owner and HQ manager accounts.
- Access by role and branch, so each person sees only what their role allows.
- An audit trail that records who changed what, and cannot be edited.
- Each terminal is registered to one branch and can be unregistered remotely.
- Staff who handle personal data are trained and bound by confidentiality.
- Regular backups and tested recovery.

If a personal data breach is likely to put you at real risk, we will notify the NPC and the people affected within 72 hours of learning about it, as NPC Circular No. 16-03 requires. For data we process for a business, we will tell that business without undue delay so it can meet its own duties.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

- **be informed** about how your personal data is used;
- **access** your personal data;
- **object** to processing, including direct marketing;
- **have it erased or blocked** when it is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
- **correct** data that is wrong or incomplete;
- **get a copy** in a common electronic format (data portability);
- **claim damages** if you are harmed by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorized use of your personal data;
- **file a complaint** with the [National Privacy Commission](https://www.privacy.gov.ph).

You can update most of your account details yourself in Owner Dashboard › Settings › Account and security. For anything else, email [privacy@aeso.ph](mailto:privacy@aeso.ph). We will confirm who you are, then reply within 15 working days.

If you are a customer of a business that uses Cleo, send your request to that business. If you contact us instead, we will pass it on to them.

Some data must be kept even after an erasure request, such as invoice copies the BIR requires. We will tell you when that applies.

## 9. Children

Cleo accounts are for people 18 and over who act for a business. A business that records customers under 18, for example in a loyalty program, is responsible for getting a parent's or guardian's consent where the law requires it.

## 10. Service messages and marketing

We send service messages about your account, security, billing and important changes. You can't turn these off while your account is active. Marketing messages about other Aeso products are sent only with your consent, and every one includes a way to stop them.

## 11. Changes to this policy

When we make an important change, we will tell you by email or in Cleo at least 15 days before it takes effect, and we will ask you to accept the new version the next time you sign in. Each version shows its effective date at the top.
