A few things
worth knowing.

Before your first sale.

What’s included in the subscription?

A software subscription includes the first licensed terminal in one branch. Additional terminals in the same branch use the plan’s extra-terminal rate. Hardware, payment-processing fees and custom services are separate.

Can I use just the Terminal?

Yes. Basic opens directly into a standalone Terminal, with no mode switcher. Its staff, business settings and billing live there too. Pro and Max add the Owner Dashboard and Back Office.

How do annual payments work?

You pay the first-terminal annual price upfront, plus 12 monthly fees for each additional terminal. Each new branch starts at its own first-terminal rate. Use the calculator above to see the total for one branch.

Can Cleo work without internet?

The Terminal is designed for offline selling, with synchronization when the connection returns. Online payment authorization and cloud services still depend on their providers and connectivity. The interactive preview uses sample data and simulated services.

Can I try Cleo before creating an account?

Yes. Explore the demo with a sample business. The demo keeps its data separate from your account and simulates sales and payments.

Is this a live payment service?

Accounts use secure sign-in through Cleo’s cloud service. Payment checkout is still a preview: provider payments, server-side licensing and production sales services need to be connected before commercial use. No real payment is taken here.

Does Cleo claim BIR accreditation?

No accreditation claim is made here. Cleo is designed to support Philippine invoicing workflows, but BIR functionality, applicable accreditation and your tax configuration must be validated before commercial use.
